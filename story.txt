Mira, a cheerful clockmaker’s apprentice, followed a trail of blue feathers into the oldest forest beyond her village.
At the forest’s heart, she discovered a sleeping mapmaker who had lost the road home.
Mira promised to repair his enchanted compass, even though a mischievous wind spirit guarded the missing needle.
With only her silver toolkit and a pocketful of patience, she stepped beneath the whispering trees to find it.

Ending

Mira reached the compass first, but the wind spirit swept it into a storm that scattered the forest’s paths.
Instead of chasing the spirit, she sang a steady song that helped the frightened trees remember where they stood.
The wind softened, placed the needle in Mira’s hand, and asked to become the mapmaker’s first student.
Together they rebuilt the paths, while Mira carried the compass home as proof that patience could calm even the wildest storm.
