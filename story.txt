Mira, a cheerful clockmaker’s apprentice, followed a trail of blue feathers into the oldest forest beyond her village.
At the forest’s heart, she discovered a sleeping mapmaker who had lost the road home.
Mira promised to repair his enchanted compass, even though a mischievous wind spirit guarded the missing needle.
With only her silver toolkit and a pocketful of patience, she stepped beneath the whispering trees to find it.
