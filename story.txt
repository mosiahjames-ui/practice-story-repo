Mira, a cheerful clockmaker’s apprentice, set out to repair the ancient bell that guided lost travelers home.
She carried a tiny silver toolkit and greeted every crow, fox, and tired wanderer along the mountain path.
A jealous fog spirit tried to hide the bell tower, fearing that restored travelers would reveal its secret kingdom.
At the foot of the tower, Mira heard a faint ticking beneath the stones and wondered whether the bell was hiding a secret of its own.

Mira’s kindness won over the spirit, and together they repaired the bell so its warm song could guide everyone safely home.
At dawn, the spirit opened the hidden kingdom and invited Mira to become its first human guardian.
Mira accepted, promising to keep the gates open for anyone who was lost or lonely.
From that day forward, the bell rang twice for travelers and once for the spirit, who finally had a home in the world above.
