She carried a tiny silver toolkit and greeted every crow, fox, and tired wanderer along the mountain path.
A jealous fog spirit tried to hide the bell tower, fearing that restored travelers would reveal its secret kingdom.
Mira’s kindness won over the spirit, and together they repaired the bell so its warm song could guide everyone safely home.
But when the bell rang, the mountain answered with a second, deeper note from beneath the snow.
The fog spirit realized it was the voice of its missing sister and led Mira to a frozen garden.
Mira rang the bell again, melting the ice and freeing the sister, whose laughter filled the valley with lanterns.
The two spirits became keepers of the road, while Mira returned to the village with a new bell for every traveler.
